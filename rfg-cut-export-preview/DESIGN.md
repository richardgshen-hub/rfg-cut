# RFG Cut Export Preview

## Style Prompt

An editorial, local-first AI editing tool rendered as a calm vertical product preview. The mood is focused and trustworthy rather than futuristic: deep charcoal workspace, warm orange decision accent, soft paper-white typography, and one muted sage confirmation color. Motion feels deliberate—captions enter as a reading aid, not as spectacle.

## Colors

- Canvas: `#111110`
- Surface: `#1D1D1B`
- Primary text: `#E9E7E2`
- Accent: `#EF7C45`
- Confirmed state: `#7EAD89`

## Typography

- Chinese UI and body: Noto Sans SC
- Timecodes and system labels: DM Mono

## What NOT to Do

- No neon blue, glassmorphism, or generic AI gradients.
- No fake analytics, score dials, or unverified accuracy claims.
- No tiny unreadable UI text.
- No fast, chaotic kinetic typography.
